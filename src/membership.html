Borrowing & Late-Return Policy

  • Late-Return Fine: A fine of PKR 30 per book per day applies after the due date (capped at PKR 1,000 per item).
  • Grace Period: A 2-day grace period is granted before overdue fines begin accruing.
  • Account Hold: Borrowing privileges are temporarily paused if overdue fines exceed PKR 300 or if an item is more than 14 days overdue.
  • Lost or Damaged Books: Members must replace the lost copy with an identical edition or pay the current retail price plus a PKR 200 processing fee.

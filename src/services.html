Digital & On-Site Services

Modern digital tools, quiet study spaces, and self-service facilities for members.

1. Online Public Access Catalogue (OPAC)

Available 24/7

Search real-time shelf availability, reserve checked-out titles, and renew active loans.

  1. Visit any kiosk terminal on the ground floor or log in with your Member ID.
  2. Search by book title, author name, ISBN, or subject tag.
  3. Click Place Hold to reserve a copy for pickup at the circulation desk.

2. E-Books & Audiobook Portal

Available 24/7

Stream or download over 15,000 digital books, academic journals, and audiobooks.

  1. Activate your digital PIN at the membership counter during registration.
  2. Sign in using your library card number and 4-digit PIN.
  3. Borrow up to 3 digital titles simultaneously for 14 days (auto-returns on expiry).

3. Quiet Study-Room Booking

9:30 AM – 7:30 PM

Sound-insulated group and solo study pods equipped with whiteboards and power outlets.

  1. Check room availability at the 2nd-floor help desk up to 48 hours in advance.
  2. Book a 2-hour slot (extendable by 1 hour if no subsequent booking exists).
  3. Present your active library card 10 minutes before your slot to collect the room key.

4. Printing, Scanning & Photocopying

10:00 AM – 7:00 PM

Self-service document printing and flatbed scanning for research notes and assignments.

  1. Upload PDF documents from a USB drive or workstation at the Print Station.
  2. Select B&W (PKR 10/page) or Color (PKR 35/page); document scanning to USB is free.
  3. Collect printouts at the media counter while respecting copyright guidelines.

5. Free High-Speed Wi-Fi

During Library Hours

Complimentary fiber-optic wireless internet across all reading halls and courtyard seating.

  1. Connect your device to the ReadersHaven-Guest wireless network.
  2. Enter your active Membership ID and registered mobile number on the login portal.
  3. Enjoy up to 4 hours of high-speed access per daily session.
